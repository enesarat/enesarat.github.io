Privacy Policy for Hapşu

Last updated: October 2026

Enes Arat ("us", "we", or "our") operates the Hapşu mobile application (the "Service").

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

1. No Account Required & Anonymity

We strongly believe in your privacy. You are not required to create an account to use Hapşu. We do not collect, store, or request personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number. Any details you optionally enter on the profile page stay on your device.

2. Health and Medication Data (Local Storage)

Hapşu allows you to track medication schedules and health logs (e.g. blood pressure, blood glucose). Your medication data, plans, dose history, health measurements and the health reports you create are stored locally on your device. We do not collect this data into an account, back it up to the cloud, or sync it. The limited data sent to our servers so that certain features can work is described item by item in Section 4.

3. Permissions

Hapşu requests the permissions below only when you use the related feature. Declining a permission does not prevent you from using the rest of the app.

  • Notifications and Alarms (including time-sensitive "Critical Alerts" and Live Activities): to show your medication reminders at the right time.
  • Camera: to read a medicine box barcode or a prescription/medicine label, and to scan Care Circle QR codes.
  • Photos: to pick a label photo or a profile photo from your gallery.
  • Location: to show pharmacies near you on the map. Your location is used on your device and is not sent to our servers.
  • Tracking (iOS only): to ask whether you allow personalized ads (see Section 6).

4. Data Sent to Our Servers

While we do not collect personal profiles, we use a secure cloud backend (Supabase) to deliver some features. None of this data is linked to your name, email address or phone number.

a) Reminders, Live Activities and Critical Alerts (iOS only)

So that Live Activities and Critical Alerts can be shown even when the app is closed, only the device's anonymous push token, a random reminder ID and the dose time are temporarily sent to the server. The medication name is not sent to the server; the name shown in the notification is added on your device. These records are deleted automatically when the reminder ends.

b) Label Reading with the Camera

When you scan a prescription or medicine label with the camera or from your gallery, the photo may in some cases be sent through our server to Google's Gemini AI service so that the text can be read correctly. This feature can only be used after you read this notice in the app and give your consent. The photo is not stored by us. Barcode scanning happens entirely on your device.

We use this service under Google's unpaid (free) terms. Under these terms Google may use the submitted photos and the extracted text to provide, improve and develop its products and services (including AI models), and this content may be read by human reviewers at Google. For this reason, if the label shows your name, national ID number or prescription number, we recommend covering them before taking the photo. Details: Gemini API Additional Terms of Service.

c) Care Circle

When you share a plan with someone close to you, the plan details (medication name, times, etc.) are transferred directly from device to device by QR code or share code. For live follow-up, only a random plan reference, dose times, whether a dose was taken or skipped, and the device's push token, platform, time zone and language are sent to the server. No medication name, diagnosis or note is sent to the server.

d) On-Duty Pharmacies

For the on-duty pharmacy list, only the province and district you select are sent to our server. Map images are loaded from OpenFreeMap; as part of normal internet traffic this service can see your IP address and the map area being viewed.

e) Feedback

If you choose to submit feedback through the app, the text, the name you optionally enter, the platform and the app version are sent securely to our servers.

5. Data Storage and Security

The security of your local data and of the limited technical data sent to our servers is strictly important to us. All communication with the server uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS). We only use trusted services necessary to operate the app.

6. Advertising

To stay free, Hapşu shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads are shown only after you complete an action such as logging a dose, saving a measurement or creating a plan, or as optional rewarded ads once the free uses of features such as the camera, barcode and reports run out. Reminders and notifications never depend on ads.

Google may process your device's advertising ID, IP address and device information to serve and measure ads and to prevent abuse. Your health and medication data is never shared with advertising providers.

  • iOS: The advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you choose "Allow" instead of "Ask App Not to Track". You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads.
  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: Your consent for personalized ads is requested in the app through Google's consent form.

How Google uses data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

7. Third Parties

We do not sell your data to anyone. We only use the following service providers to operate the Service:

  • Supabase: server infrastructure (the data in Section 4).
  • Google AdMob: advertising (Section 6).
  • Google Gemini: label reading; content may be used by Google to improve its services (4-b).
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service: delivering notifications.
  • OpenFreeMap: map images (4-d).

8. Children's Privacy

Hapşu is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Deletion of Your Data

Hapşu allows you to delete your stored data through the following methods:

a) Local Data on Your Device

The following data types are stored only on your device and can be deleted directly within the app:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone, address): You can clear these by editing your profile page.
  • Medication records and plans: You can delete each medication individually.
  • Dose history (taken/skipped logs): Automatically cleared when the related medication is deleted.
  • Profile photo: You can remove it from your profile page.
  • All app data: Uninstalling the app from your device permanently deletes all local data.

b) Data Stored on Servers

The following data may be temporarily stored on our servers:

  • Reminder records and push notification tokens (iOS): Automatically deleted when the related reminder ends.
  • Care Circle records: You can end sharing within the app; you can request deletion of any remaining records by email.
  • Feedback messages: Deleted upon request.

To request the deletion of your data from our servers, please send an email to artcodens@gmail.com. Your deletion request will be processed within 30 days.

10. Changes To This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us by email: artcodens@gmail.com
